# Cover letter — a possible collaboration on agentic services (severable, non-binding)

**From:** the MONARK team (Shōgen is our analysis/measurement component).
**To:** the Pocket Network maintainers.
**Date:** 2026-09-29.
**Status:** **severable and entirely optional.** This letter is deliberately kept **separate from,
and out of, our security disclosure**. Nothing here is a condition of that disclosure, and the
disclosure asks for nothing in return.

---

Dear Pocket Network team,

Under separate cover we have sent you a private, good-faith security disclosure regarding poktroll
at v0.1.35. That report stands entirely on its own; please read it independently of this letter and
without any expectation attached to it. We are long-time members of the Pocket community and our
first purpose was simply to help you strengthen the protocol.

Separately — and only if it is of interest to you — we would like to open a conversation about a
possible collaboration on **agentic services**. MONARK operates an agentic verification and
measurement harness (attestation, gating, calibration) that we expose as an **MCP service**. Pocket
Network, as a decentralized, permissionless data-supply substrate, is a natural fit for the kind of
independent, replayable source-measurement we build. We think there is interesting ground to explore
together, at whatever pace and shape suits you.

We want to be transparent about one self-imposed constraint that shapes any such collaboration. As
long as MONARK publishes any measurement *about* Pocket, we hold ourselves to a **neutrality
commitment**: we will not take revenue that originates from Pocket while such a measurement is
published, so that our measurements remain visibly independent and credible. Any collaboration we
propose would be structured to respect that commitment (for example, by scoping engagements so they
do not create Pocket-origin revenue while a Pocket measurement is live, or by sequencing the two).
We would rather name this up front than have it discovered later.

We are also mindful that unsolicited proposals sent to you may fall under the feedback terms of your
Terms of Use. We have kept this collaboration proposal **out of the security disclosure** precisely
so that the disclosure is not entangled with any business discussion, and we are happy to agree on
appropriate terms for any collaboration before exchanging anything substantive.

If this is of interest, we would welcome a short conversation to see whether there is a fit. If it
is not, that is entirely fine — the security disclosure needs no reply beyond your handling of the
findings, and we will continue to support your remediation regardless.

With respect and appreciation for your work,

**The MONARK team**
